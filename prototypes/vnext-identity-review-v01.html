CHAPTER 01 · A PLACE BETWEEN

Under the willow light

The last light of the evening settled softly over the fields, and for a moment, every familiar path felt like a new beginning.

She opened the book where the folded page waited. Between the ink and the quiet, a whole world was holding its breath.

She stayed a little longer. Some places were not meant to be hurried through.
